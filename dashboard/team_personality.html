The question

Does one style of play win more titles? Every team picks a pace and a shot diet. Those two habits sort teams into four personalities.

Pace

Possessions per game. Fast or slow?

Shot selection

Share of shots that are threes. Shooters take more, attackers take fewer and score mostly on twos.

Both are measured against that season's average team.

How it was made

The process

I wanted habits that show how a team plays, not how good it is. Pace and three-point share barely track scoring margin (correlations and ), so the corners are personalities, not rankings.

The reflection

A plain scatter felt abstract, so the chart became a court. Color is saved for March: champions and Final Four teams. Nine tournaments is a small sample, so read this as a pattern, not a rule.

Method

  • team-seasons: D1 teams with 15+ games against D1 opponents.
  • Pace = estimated possessions per game (Dean Oliver). Three-point share = 3PA ÷ FGA.
  • games with impossible possession counts removed. All Final Four teams kept.
  • No tournament in 2019–20. A few extreme teams are drawn at the map's edge.

Sources

  • NCAA Women's Basketball Data Files 2026 (ESPN), the official Love Data Week 2027 dataset.
  • Built with scripts/01_clean.py and scripts/08_build_team_personality.py.
